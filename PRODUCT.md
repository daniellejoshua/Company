# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and decision-makers at growing businesses who need tailored software to improve operations, launch new digital services, or replace systems that no longer fit their workflows.

## Product Purpose

JADE is a custom-software agency that designs and develops business management systems, MLM platforms, point-of-sale systems, e-commerce platforms, and mobile, desktop, and web applications. The website should establish trust, communicate JADE's capabilities clearly, and help qualified businesses begin a project conversation.

## Positioning

JADE builds software around each client's actual business needs rather than forcing those needs into a generic product. Its breadth across operational systems and customer-facing applications lets clients work with one partner from idea through delivery.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients use the marketing website to understand JADE's services, assess the quality of its work, review trust signals, and decide whether to start a project inquiry.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The website is implemented in Next.js with TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Motion, Lucide React, and Embla Carousel.
- The landing page is being implemented section by section. Header, hero, social proof, and partner carousel are the current scope.
- The experience must remain responsive and performant across desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.
- Approved partner logos and client portraits are not currently present and must not be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments

- Company name: JADE, representing Arvie, Jhon, Danielle, and Eman.
- Brand line: People · Ideas · Technology · Real Solutions.
- The established identity uses white, emerald, and deep forest green with a professional, modern, and reliable voice.
- Existing logo, device mockup, and geometric artwork in `public/assets/` are the approved visual assets.

## Evidence on Hand

- JADE logo asset: `public/assets/logo/jadeLogo.png`.
- Product dashboard mockup: `public/assets/hero/DeviceMockup.png`.
- JADE geometric artwork: `public/assets/hero/hero-shape.svg`.
- The user approved the website copy "100+ projects delivered" and the six partner names in `src/data/partners.ts`.
- Partner logo files, client portraits, testimonials, and supporting project records are not present in the repository and must not be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show how tailored software supports real business operations.
2. Build trust through approved evidence rather than generic claims or fabricated proof.
3. Make starting a project conversation obvious and low-friction.
4. Keep the experience polished, direct, responsive, and accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, descriptive image alternatives, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion behavior.
